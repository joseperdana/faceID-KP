# Organization Context: Komisi Pemuda GKI Bromo Malang (KP GKI Bromo Malang)

## 1. Organization Overview
* **Organization Name:** Youth Commission of Indonesian Christian Church Bromo Malang (KP GKI Bromo Malang)
* **Target Demographic:** College-aged youth congregation at GKI Bromo Malang.
* **Core Purpose:** A platform for the youth congregation to fellowship, worship, and grow in their faith in God.
* **Vision:** To become God's partners who transform the youth of GKI Bromo into God-fearing individuals with a proper Christian self-concept, a clear life vision, unwavering integrity amidst real-life pressures, and the independence to make a positive impact on others and their environment.

## 2. Operational Details
* **Fellowship Schedule:** Every Saturday at 17:00 WIB
* **Location:** Multipurpose Building, GKI Bromo Malang
* **Average Attendance:** 70 - 100 people per week
* **Committee Size:** 34 active members
* **Committee Structure:**
  * 2 Advisors (Pembina)
  * 1 Chairperson (Ketua)
  * 1 Treasurer (Bendahara)
  * 1 Secretary (Sekretaris)
  * Fellowship Division (Sie Persekutuan)
  * Care & Attention Division (Sie Pemerhati)
  * Multimedia Division (Sie Multimedia)

## 3. Weekly Fellowship Sequence (Order of Service)
1. **Face Recognition Attendance** (Absen Wajah KP)
2. **Pre-service Activities** (Led by Care & Attention Division)
3. **Praise and Worship** (Sequence: 1 Worship song > 3 Praise songs > 1 Worship song)
4. **Opening Prayer for the Word**
5. **The Word / Sermon** (Format varies: lecture, discussion, sharing, games, etc.)
6. **Closing Prayer for the Word**
7. **Intercessory Prayer** (Doa Syafaat)
8. **Welcoming Newcomers**
9. **Announcements**
10. **Annual Theme Song**

## 4. Technical System Context: Face Recognition Attendance
* **System Name:** Absen Wajah KP (FaceID-KP)
* **System Architecture:** Python and Google Sheets integration.
* **Usage Timing:** Executed immediately when a congregation member first enters the fellowship area.
* **Primary Objective:** To assist the committee in automating and streamlining the congregation attendance recording process, enabling rapid tracking of congregation statistics.
* **Required Data & Statistics:**
  * Total weekly attendance count.
  * Individual attendee data mapping (Name, Phone Number, Gender).
  * Weekly gender ratio comparisons.
  * Tracking and identification of first-time attendees (newcomers).
  * Tracking of members with prolonged absences (retention monitoring).
  * Extensibility for future statistical analytics as needed by the committee.
